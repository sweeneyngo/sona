    How to use sona
    
          
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Workflow
    
            
      
              
    1. Drop a song on the start screen. sona suggests its tempo; check it (½× if it
                sounds twice too fast), then press Transcribe.
      2. 
              
    2. Play (Space) to hear the singer against the notes, and fix what's wrong.
      3. 
              
    3. Add lyrics (Edit → Lyrics…) and shape the pitch (below).
      4. 
              
    4. Export to OpenUtau (USTX), UTAU (UST) or MIDI. File → Save session
                keeps everything for later; the page also autosaves.
      5. 
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    What the colours mean
    
            
    
              low  high confidence
               flagged: worth checking
               kept off the grid
               out of key
            
    
            
    The white line is the singer's pitch; the orange curve is what the export will sing.
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Editing
    
            
    
              Drag a note to move it (time + pitch), drag its right edge to resize,
              double-click empty space to add, select and press Delete to remove. Hover or
              click a note to see its confidence sub-scores.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Pitch
    
            
    
              With pitch curve on, the selected note shows its pitch handles on the
              orange curve. Circles are portamento points: drag one to move it, double-click
              the curve to add one, Alt-click or hover and press Delete to remove one. Diamonds
              shape the vibrato: drag where it starts, the top corners for depth and fades,
              the lower one for its period. The bar under the roll takes exact values.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    From a song
    
            
    
              Transcribing a song needs the page opened from sona serve, which runs
              the vocal separators and the note engines on this computer. More options on
              the start screen picks the separation, the note engine and a triplet grid.
              The tempo and grid of a loaded song are under the ♩ button.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Lyrics and UTAU export
    
            
    
              Select a note and press L to type its lyric:
              Enter or Tab saves it and
              moves to the next note (Shift+Tab
              the previous), Esc stops. あ Lyrics pastes a
              whole text at once. UST (UTAU) and USTX (OpenUtau) export the
              same snapped notes as MIDI, with lyrics; a note without one sings あ. The
              notes move onto the editor's grid, so the vocal starts up to one grid cell
              earlier than the stem (the status line says how much).
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Pitch: portamento and vibrato
    
            
    
              〰 Fit pitch reads each note's glide and vibrato from the stem's pitch
              track (the white line). The orange curve is what the exported notes will
              sing in OpenUtau. Select a note to edit its glide and vibrato in the bar under
              the roll, or press V to toggle its vibrato. UST and USTX
              export carry them (USTX pitch points and vibrato; UST PBS/PBW/PBY/PBM and VBR).
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Navigation
    
            
    
              The roll shows ~2 octaves at a time. Scroll to move pitch, Shift-scroll to
              move time, ⌘/Ctrl-scroll to zoom (double-click the ruler to reset). Click the
              top ruler to seek; drag it to shift the grid phase.
              Shift-click the ruler on the beat you hear as beat 1:
              that line becomes a bar line. Bright lines are bars, numbered as OpenUtau
              will number them; every export puts OpenUtau's bars and beats on these lines.
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    Keyboard
    
            
    
              
    Space play / pause
    
              
    Ctrl + Z undo
    
              
    Ctrl + ⇧ + Z redo
    
              
    ←→ seek a beat
    
              
    Shift + ←→ seek a bar
    
              
    ↑↓ move pitch a semitone
    
              
    Shift + ↑↓ move an octave
    
              
    +− zoom in / out
    
              
    Home jump to start
    
              
    0 reset the view
    
              
    Del delete selected note
    
              
    L type the selected note's lyric
    
              
    V vibrato on / off for the selected note
    
              
    ? this help
    
            
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Settings
    
          
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Annotation / session JSON
    
            
            
    The core's annotation, or a saved session to resume editing.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Vocal stem (audio)
    
            
            
    Played against the synthesized notes. Any format the browser decodes.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Flag threshold 0.50
    
            
            
    Notes below this confidence are outlined red for review. Calibrate by ear per song.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Musical key —
    
            
            
    The key is auto-detected from the notes. Off: out-of-key notes are outlined for review. On: every note is snapped to the nearest scale tone (display + export). Purely interpretation — it never changes what was detected.
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Lyrics
    
          
        
    
        
    
          
    
            Paste the lyrics. They're split into one token per note: kana by mora
            (きゃ is one; ー, っ and ん are their own), romaji and English by word
            (write romaji morae with spaces: sa ku ra), each kanji on its
            own (paste kana for best results). Tokens fill notes in time order.
